About

About Vortix VPN.

Vortix VPN is an Android VPN app built for users who want a simple, totally free, unlimited and ad-free way to browse public VPN server options, support private browsing, and connect without creating an account.

Our purpose

Many VPN apps are difficult to understand before a user connects. Vortix VPN focuses on a clearer Android VPN experience: users can open the app, review available public VPN server options, start a secure VPN connection, and see the current connection status. The app is simple, totally free, unlimited, and ad-free.

What the app does

The app works as a VPN client. It helps Android users connect to selected public VPN Gate/OpenVPN servers. Public VPN server availability can change because many servers are operated by volunteers or third parties. This means speed, location availability, and connection reliability can vary from day to day.

Transparency

Vortix VPN does not ask users to create an in-app account. The website provides the app privacy policy, terms of service, support contact, and educational VPN guides so users can understand the service before installing or using it.
