Android privacy

Android VPN privacy tips.

A VPN is one useful privacy tool, but it works best together with sensible Android and browser habits.

Watch the VPN indicator

Android shows a VPN indicator when a VPN tunnel is active. If the indicator is gone, check the app before assuming the VPN is still connected.

Review app permissions

Only grant app permissions that make sense for the app you are using. A VPN app normally needs VPN permission, but unrelated apps should not receive sensitive permissions without a clear reason.

Use private browser settings carefully

Private browsing modes can reduce local history storage, but they do not make you invisible online. Websites, accounts, cookies, and the VPN endpoint can still affect privacy.

Keep Android updated

Security updates help fix vulnerabilities. Keep your Android system, browser, and important apps updated whenever possible.

Avoid logging into sensitive accounts on unknown networks

A VPN can help on public Wi-Fi, but you should still avoid risky links, suspicious downloads, and unknown login pages.
